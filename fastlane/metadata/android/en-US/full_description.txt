PhoneToLinux seamlessly bridges your Android smartphone with your Linux desktop over your local network.

Key Features:
- Make and receive voice calls from your desktop.
- Read and send SMS messages directly from your computer.
- Real-time notification forwarding and contact synchronization.
- File access and transfer via embedded WebDAV server.
- Complete Privacy: All communication occurs strictly over your local Wi-Fi network without any cloud servers.

For full functionality, a companion application for Fedora Linux is required, available via COPR repository:
#> dnf copr enable stanislav1988/phonetolinuxdesktop
