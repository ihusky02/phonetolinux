# Privacy Policy for PhoneToLinux

**Effective date:** September 6, 2026

Stanisław Tlołka ("we", "our", or "us") built the **PhoneToLinux** app as an open-source companion tool. This SERVICE is provided by Stanisław Tlołka and is intended for use as-is.

### Information Collection and Use
To deliver its core functionality—bridging your Android smartphone with a Linux desktop environment—the app accesses local device data, including:
* **SMS Messages & Call Logs:** Used solely to display and forward notifications or details of incoming calls and messages to your paired Linux machine over your local network.
* **Contacts:** Used to resolve caller and sender names for the local Linux desktop interface.

### Data Sharing and Security
* **We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external or third-party servers.** 
* All communication is handled locally between your Android device and your personal Linux computer.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
